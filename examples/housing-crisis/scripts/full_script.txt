THE HOUSING CRISIS ISN'T AN ACCIDENT
5 slides
======================================================================

[SLIDE 01 — THE HOUSING CRISIS ISN'T AN ACCIDENT]
Housing didn't become unaffordable by accident.
It became unaffordable through thousands of specific choices: what we allowed builders to build, what we encouraged banks to finance, and what we told ourselves about our own neighborhoods.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 02 — SIX FORCES BEHIND THE SHORTAGE]
Six forces, all reinforcing each other.
Restrictive land rules limit supply. Cheap credit increases demand for the same limited land.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 03 — THREE WAYS TO THINK ABOUT HOUSING]
Ask someone what a house is and you'll get three answers.
A place to live. An investment. A piece of infrastructure.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 04 — THE NUMBERS THAT MATTER]
Here's a habit worth building.
Never accept a housing chart without checking the time window and the geography.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 05 — WHAT CAN ACTUALLY CHANGE?]
There is no magic policy.
There is a toolbox: build more in the right places, decompress the investment demand, protect tenants, and make land speculation less attractive.

[PAUSE]
