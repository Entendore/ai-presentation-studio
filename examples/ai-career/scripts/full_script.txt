[SLIDE 01 — AI WON'T REPLACE YOU. A PERSON USING AI MIGHT.]
Ignore the headline that says AI will replace your entire profession.
Jobs are bundles of tasks. Some of those tasks automate fast. Others barely move.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 02 — AUTOMATABLE VS. DURABLE]
Run this test on your own week.
How much time goes into producing a first draft, reformatting information, or looking things up?

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 03 — FIVE SKILLS THAT COMPOUND]
These six skills get more valuable as the tools get cheaper.
Because when everyone can generate a draft, the scarce skill is knowing which draft is good.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 04 — YOUR 30-DAY PLAN]
Don't take a course. Don't read ten hot takes.
Pick one task you dislike. Automate it. Then build a way to tell whether the output is actually correct.

[PAUSE]
