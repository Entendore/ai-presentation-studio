======================================================================
YOU'RE NOT LAZY. YOU'RE EXHAUSTED.
4 slides
======================================================================

[SLIDE 01 — YOU'RE NOT LAZY.]
If you've spent a year trying to fix yourself with a better morning routine, hear this.
Exhaustion is not a character flaw.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 02 — REST IS NOT THE PROBLEM]
There's tired you can sleep off.
And there's tired that survives a full night's sleep because the thing draining you is still there on Monday.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 03 — SLEEP ≠ RECOVERY]
Two people can sleep the same seven hours and wake up completely different.
One feels safe. One is bracing.

[PAUSE]

[SLIDE 04 — THREE EXPERIMENTS]
Try three small things.
Track when you actually feel depleted, not just when you feel sleepy.

[PAUSE]
